They cover callback and ownership contracts, recorded interpolation/exact starts,
mission lifecycle/status/fingerprint handling, the native-step and presentation-pitch
boundaries, library immutability and validation, activation rollback, and DCS Lua
storage behavior including locked log files and void-success file I/O.

New envelope regressions first failed on the former altitude/rate caps. The final
checks accept a 180-degree/s roll and a modeled 7.5-G / 350-knot level turn at
multiple recorded MSL coordinates. Interpolation uses spherical rotation except
for almost-identical samples. These are mathematical path checks, not proof of
simulator G load, afterburner state, AGL clearance or ground contact.

Both previously rejected real recordings (80.92 and 69.38 seconds) passed actual
library validation, conversion, native evaluation at 100 Hz and generated mission
requirements/routes/configuration checks. Original file hashes were unchanged.
The live companion API reports all three recent completed takes Ready.

## Measured live evidence

- Accepted baseline: 38.78 seconds / 1,940 samples. Earlier matched jitter analysis
  reduced mean correction from 18.84 to 1.88 cm and p95 from 27.50 to 3.23 cm.
- Stock staging: zero measured held-player drift over 691 samples; initial spacing
  45.714 m versus the 45.72 m target. Three-second countdown plus 0.45-second trigger
  delay established why native playback must anchor to actual release.
- Recorded staging: all 1,940 samples completed, first/last commands matched the
  tape, first native position readback was within 6.93 mm, velocity matched at
  logged precision, hook restored and player telemetry continued after removal.
- Automatic save: a 39.48-second / 1,975-sample take saved 9 ms after Stop with
  byte verification. Storage checks and DCS log-history capture fixed earlier failures.
- Nose-jump fix: native presentation pitch was added to commanded attitude.
  The user confirmed the correction removed the jump; measured presentation error
  fell from about 10.07 degrees to 0.009 degrees allowing one sample of timing offset.
  The final controller retains the correction without temporary diagnostics.
- Final hard-turn run, DCS process 21832: 4,047 successful motion writes from
  7.801 to 88.721 simulation seconds, the entire 80.92-second tape. All 4,046
  pre-integration hook calls recorded before the final write succeeded. Completion
  and hook restoration are present in the object log; no rejected motion statuses.
  Commanded angular rate reached about 153.67 degrees/s and altitude reached
  572.47 m MSL. Excluding initial spawn correction, mean position correction was
  2.23 cm, p95 4.13 cm and maximum 5.95 cm; velocity write readback matched exactly
  at logged precision. This is a different, harder maneuver, not a matched-window
  comparison with the baseline. Corrections are not absolute rendered accuracy.

## Manual acceptance and remaining scope

The user reported the final replay worked great and confirmed all other earlier
requested checks passed. This includes the requested repeated/longer-take, restart,
real pause/resume and multiple-frame-rate checks. Exact additional take durations,
frame-rate settings and per-condition telemetry were not supplied; those results
are recorded as user-reported acceptance, not independent measurements.

Playback completion does not stop the mission or call an independent recorder's
Stop operation; the lifecycle contract is also covered by the Lua harness. Actual
layered recording/playback remains a later feature.

No full ground-to-ground demo claim is made. Stationary/taxi speed, ground staging,
gear/flaps, afterburner effects/sound, touchdown/rollout, contact/collision/wake,
full-demo durations, other aircraft/builds and portable installation remain under
existing follow-up tickets. Source and compact summaries are published; raw logs,
recordings, game assets, generated packages and user media remain local.
