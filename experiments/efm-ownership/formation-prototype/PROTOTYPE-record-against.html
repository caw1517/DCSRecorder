Recording a position while a formation plays

PROTOTYPE, throwaway. Question (issue 41): how does the player record a new take for one formation position while the other positions play? The proposal you can click through: you choose the position to fly in the companion; you can also mute positions there. The prepared copy contains only the playing aircraft and you, because muted and take-less authored aircraft are removed. At the countdown release, every playing aircraft and your recording start on one shared epoch, which becomes your take's time zero. F10 Stop saves your take as a new file, which records what it was flown against. The companion then offers a new formation version with your take in that position. Existing takes and earlier versions are never changed.
