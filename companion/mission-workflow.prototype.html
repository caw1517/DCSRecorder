Throwaway workflow prototype · reviewed design

Your mission, one recording, a prepared copy

Can you keep designing in DCS Mission Editor, use the companion only to select aircraft and recordings, and reliably replay a flight after the mission has been edited?

This is an in-memory demonstration, not a working DCS integration. Aircraft, files and recordings below are examples. Ground operation and change detection still need implementation and simulator validation. Reloading resets everything.

Free play

Try actions in any order. Editing represents saving the authored mission in Mission Editor; preparing always creates a new copy.

Guided walkthroughs

Proposed preparation contract

  • The original authored mission and recordings stay unchanged.
  • The recording is tied to a saved mission version and its selected aircraft.
  • Playback starts at the first recorded pose, not a relocated editor marker.
  • The other selected Hornet becomes your flyable aircraft at its authored position.
  • Unrelated aircraft, trucks, briefing, triggers and resources are preserved.
  • Each generated copy appears in Saved Games/DCS/Missions.

Changes needing a decision

  • Moved recorded aircraft: proposed hard stop when using the edited mission. Use the saved version or restore its placement.
  • Moved player aircraft or scenery: proposed change review, followed by explicit use of that scene.
  • Different terrain or ambiguous aircraft identity: proposed hard stop.
  • Prepared copies: edit the authored mission and prepare again. Runtime detection of edits to generated copies is still unproven.

Outside this model

How the aircraft is held, released and kept parked belongs to the next lifecycle decision. This file does not simulate DCS pause, contact, collision, trigger execution, scenery safety or additional playback layers.

Direct editor-only setup remains an alternative to review; the current repository does not implement that workflow.
